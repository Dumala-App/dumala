Dumala

Investor and Internal Beta Brief

A Philippines-first football team management and player-development platform built for coaches.

Beta Brief v2 June 21, 2026 Local-first public beta

Executive summary

Grassroots, amateur, and academy football operations are fragmented across spreadsheets, chat applications, notebooks, and isolated tools. Dumala consolidates the weekly coaching cycle into one football-specific workspace.

The current beta connects roster management, scheduling, match preparation, results, standings, tactics, training, discipline, bulletins, and structured FPRDS player assessment.

Near-term thesis: trusted weekly operations plus explainable player assessment create the foundation for offline adoption, shared clubs, and sustainable academy workflows.

Current beta capabilities

Club operations

Onboarding, roster, staff, scheduling, results, standings, bulletins, discipline, and awards.

Match preparation

Availability, opponent context, coach notes, tactical roles, and position-aware lineup planning.

Training

An 85-drill library with practical guides, filters, presets, and weekly calendar synchronization.

FPRDS beta

Full and Quick assessments with age and role benchmarks, OVR, history, and development plans.

Data portability

Static deployment, local browser persistence, and JSON backup/restore including assessments.

Feedback loop

In-app feedback and a public product roadmap shaped by coach testing.

Strategic roadmap

Current Beta: Reliability, recovery, validation, accessibility, and FPRDS benchmark review.
Offline and Tablet: Installable PWA, offline workflows, tablet UI, and Google Play packaging.
Cloud and Collaboration: Accounts, durable workspaces, synchronization, roles, and activity history.
Public Profiles and Drafting: Personalized links, controlled publishing, candidate comparison, shortlists, and trials.
Philippine Coach Development: Official PFF/AFC/FIFA resources, qualifications, education planning, journals, and portfolios.
Club Expansion: Attendance, reports, season archives, multiple squads, and continuity.

Potential packaging

  • Coach: Single-team operations, training, tactics, and assessments.
  • Club: Shared staff, roles, cloud synchronization, and reporting.
  • Academy: Multiple squads, expanded FPRDS reporting, drafting, and long-term development history.

Pricing and packaging remain hypotheses until validated with beta users.

Current risks

  • Browser-only data without accounts or shared ownership.
  • Incomplete full-workspace backup coverage.
  • FPRDS benchmarks still require practitioner validation.
  • Large frontend bundle and no automated regression suite.
  • Public profiles will require consent, authorization, and moderation.
