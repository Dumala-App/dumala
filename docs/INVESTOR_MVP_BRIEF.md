# Dumala: Investor and Internal Beta Brief

**Positioning:** A Philippines-first football team management and player-development platform built for coaches.

**Version:** Beta Brief v2

**Date:** 2026-06-21

## Executive summary

Grassroots, amateur, and academy football operations are fragmented across spreadsheets, chat applications, notebooks, and isolated tools. Dumala consolidates the weekly coaching cycle into one football-specific workspace.

The current beta connects roster management, scheduling, match preparation, results, standings, tactics, training, discipline, bulletins, and structured FPRDS player assessment. This gives Dumala two complementary foundations: a recurring club-operations workflow and explainable player-development records.

## Problem and opportunity

- **Fragmented administration:** Team information is difficult to maintain and recover.
- **Disconnected coaching work:** Scheduling, training, tactics, and match records rarely share context.
- **Subjective player evaluation:** Development observations are difficult to repeat or compare.
- **Limited tools for smaller clubs:** Professional workflows are usually too expensive or complex for grassroots teams.

Dumala can serve the Philippine grassroots and academy market first, then expand through offline access, tablets, cloud collaboration, public profiles, and structured coach development.

## Current beta capabilities

- Team onboarding, identity, roster, staff, and player profiles
- Match scheduling, availability, opponent context, coach notes, and results
- Automatic standings and player-stat updates
- Discipline and Man of the Match histories
- Position-aware tactics and lineup planning
- Weekly training with 52 drills, guides, filters, and presets
- FPRDS Full and Quick assessments
- Role- and age-specific benchmark scoring, OVR, history, and development plans
- Bulletins, feedback, and JSON backup/restore
- Static deployment with no backend operating cost during beta

## Primary user

The initial user is a football coach, manager, academy evaluator, or team administrator operating one team.

## Differentiation

1. **Football-specific workflow:** Operations, tactics, training, and development coexist in one product.
2. **Local-first speed:** Teams can test the product without accounts or infrastructure.
3. **Explainable assessment:** FPRDS retains raw measurements and benchmark-derived scores.
4. **Philippines-first direction:** Future coach education will reference official PFF, AFC, and FIFA resources without inventing an unofficial coaching grade.
5. **Progressive architecture:** Offline use precedes cloud synchronization, and cloud identity precedes public profiles.

## Strategic roadmap

### Current Beta: Reliability

Harden workflows, standings, result cascades, backups, validation, accessibility, and recovery.

### FPRDS Beta

Validate benchmarks and scoring, improve historical comparison, and produce downloadable assessment and development reports.

### Offline and tablet

Build an installable offline-ready PWA, optimize tablet workflows, and package the validated experience for Google Play.

### Cloud and collaboration

Add accounts, durable club workspaces, synchronization, invitations, roles, and activity history.

### Public profiles and drafting

Offer controlled personalized player and team links, richer football profiles, candidate comparison, shortlists, and trial workflows.

### Philippine coach development

Add an official-resource knowledge base, qualification tracking, education planning, journals, renewal reminders, and controlled coaching portfolios.

### Club expansion

Support attendance, season archives, reports, multiple squads, and continuity across seasons.

The detailed roadmap is maintained in [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md) and the in-app Roadmap Snapshot.

## Monetization direction

Potential packaging should be validated with beta users:

- **Coach:** Single-team operations, training, tactics, and assessments.
- **Club:** Shared staff, roles, cloud synchronization, and reporting.
- **Academy:** Multiple squads, expanded FPRDS reporting, drafting, and long-term player-development history.

No pricing or packaging is considered final.

## Current risks

- Data remains tied to one browser unless exported.
- There is no authentication or shared ownership.
- Full backup does not yet include all tactics and feature-local state.
- FPRDS benchmarks require continued practitioner validation.
- The frontend bundle needs code splitting.
- Public profiles require strong consent, authorization, and moderation controls.

## Closing

Dumala's near-term opportunity is earning trust as the practical operating and development workspace for football coaches. Reliable weekly use and explainable player assessment create the foundation for offline adoption, shared clubs, public football profiles, and sustainable academy workflows.
