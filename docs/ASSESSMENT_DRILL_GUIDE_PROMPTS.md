# Assessment drill guide prompts

This file lists every drill players may need to perform in the FPRDS Assessment Center, including:
- What it measures
- Coach-facing description (concise)
- An AI image prompt you can paste into your model

Prompt style assumptions:
- “Sports coaching illustration / training diagram”
- Clean layout, cones/markers clearly visible, minimal background clutter
- No brand logos, no readable text inside the image

---

## Base drills (all players)

### 1) 10m / 30m Sprint Test
**Measures:** Pace / acceleration (timed sprint)  
**Description:** Athlete sprints from a standing start through 10m and 30m markers. Record 10m split and 30m total time; best of 3 attempts.  
**AI prompt:** Sports coaching illustration, soccer training field, straight 30-meter sprint lane with cones labeled START, 10m, 30m, athlete sprinting forward, coach holding stopwatch, clear distance markers on ground, clean minimal infographic style, bright daylight, no text in image, high clarity, instructional diagram.

### 2) Yo-Yo Intermittent Recovery Test
**Measures:** Stamina / endurance (distance accumulated)  
**Description:** Athlete runs repeated 20m shuttles following beep timing until failure; record total distance achieved in meters.  
**AI prompt:** Instructional sports diagram, soccer field with two parallel lines 20 meters apart marked by cones, athlete running shuttle back and forth, coach with speaker/phone playing beep test, simple arrows showing shuttle direction, minimal clean infographic style, no text in image.

### 3) Illinois Agility Test
**Measures:** Agility / change of direction (time)  
**Description:** Athlete runs a standard Illinois agility cone course with weaving and turns; record completion time (best of 2).  
**AI prompt:** Coaching infographic illustration, top-down view of Illinois agility test cone layout, rectangular course with cones and center slalom cones, athlete path shown as arrows/curved line, coach timing with stopwatch, clean minimal diagram, no text in image.

### 4) Standing Broad Jump + Medicine Ball Throw
**Measures:** Strength / power (distance)  
**Description:** Broad jump: two-foot takeoff from a line, measure best distance. Medicine ball throw: consistent technique (chest or overhead), measure best distance.  
**AI prompt:** Sports coaching illustration split-panel: left panel standing broad jump with takeoff line and landing measurement tape, athlete jumping forward; right panel medicine ball throw from a marked line with measuring tape on ground, coach observing, clean instructional style, no text in image.

### 5) Rebound Board Control / Wall Pass Grid
**Measures:** First touch / ball control (successes out of 10)  
**Description:** Athlete receives rebound passes and must control the ball inside a 2m x 2m grid; record successful controls out of 10.  
**AI prompt:** Soccer training diagram, athlete facing rebound board or wall, ball rebounding back, a 2m x 2m control grid marked with tape or cones on ground, coach counting reps on clipboard, clean minimal instructional style, no text in image.

### 6) Target Gate Passing (10m)
**Measures:** Short passing accuracy (successes out of 10)  
**Description:** Athlete passes through a 1-meter cone gate from 10 meters, 10 attempts; count successful passes.  
**AI prompt:** Soccer coaching infographic, athlete passing ball toward a 1-meter cone gate 10 meters away, cones clearly visible, straight passing line, coach tracking attempts, clean diagram style, no text in image.

## Forwards / Wingers drills

### 7) Finishing (Box) — 6-ball rapid fire
**Measures:** Finishing ability (goals + on-target out of 6)  
**Description:** Six shots from marked spots around the box in quick sequence; record goals and shots on target out of 6.  
**AI prompt:** Soccer finishing drill illustration, penalty box with 6 balls placed at marked positions, athlete running onto each ball to shoot, goal in background, cones marking shot spots, coach recording goals/on-target, clean instructional diagram, no text in image.

### 8) Shot Power / Long Shots — max controlled distance
**Measures:** Shot power with control (max distance)  
**Description:** Athlete shoots from increasing distances; determine the furthest distance where the shot remains controlled and on target; record max meters.  
**AI prompt:** Soccer training diagram, goal with distance markers at 20m, 30m, 40m using cones, athlete shooting from far distance, arrows showing shot trajectories, coach measuring furthest controlled on-target distance, clean infographic style, no text in image.

### 9) Offensive 1v1 — attack channel
**Measures:** Dribbling / take-ons (successes out of 10)  
**Description:** Attacker tries to beat a defender in a narrow channel and create an end product; 10 attempts; record successful take-ons.  
**AI prompt:** Soccer 1v1 drill illustration, rectangular channel marked with cones, attacker dribbling ball facing defender, arrow showing attacking direction toward goal/end line, coach counting attempts, clean minimal coaching diagram, no text in image.

### 10) Attacking Heading — target zones
**Measures:** Heading accuracy (out of 10)  
**Description:** Ten served crosses; athlete heads toward goal/target zone; record on-target or target-zone headers out of 10.  
**AI prompt:** Soccer heading drill diagram, wide player serving a cross, striker jumping to head ball toward goal, cones marking target zone in goal area, coach with clipboard tracking reps, clean instructional style, no text in image.

## Midfield drills

### 11) Long Passing — 30m/40m into 5×5m target zone
**Measures:** Long passing accuracy (out of 5 at each distance)  
**Description:** Five passes to a 5×5m target at 30m and five at 40m; record successes out of 5 for each.  
**AI prompt:** Soccer long passing drill illustration, athlete striking long pass toward a square 5x5 target zone marked by cones, two stations shown with distance markers (30m and 40m), coach counting successful passes, clean diagram style, no text in image.

### 12) Vision / Playmaking — 4v2 rondo coach score
**Measures:** Vision / decision-making (coach score 1–10)  
**Description:** 4v2 rondo in a marked grid; coach evaluates scanning, timing, line-breaking decisions; assigns 1–10 score.  
**AI prompt:** Soccer rondo drill illustration, top-down view of a 10x10 grid with cones, four attackers passing around two defenders in middle, coach observing with clipboard, clean coaching infographic style, no text in image.

### 13) Ball Retention — 3×3m shielding
**Measures:** Ball protection under pressure (best time in seconds)  
**Description:** Attacker shields ball inside 3×3m grid against defender; record best time retained from 3 attempts.  
**AI prompt:** Soccer shielding drill diagram, small 3x3 square marked by cones, attacker shielding ball with body between defender and ball, coach with stopwatch, clean minimal instructional style, no text in image.

### 14) Pressing / Interceptions — 3v1 pressing cage
**Measures:** Pressing / ball wins (wins out of 5)  
**Description:** Three keepers vs one presser in a small grid; record how many rounds the presser wins the ball (out of 5).  
**AI prompt:** Soccer pressing drill illustration, small square grid with cones, three players passing ball around one pressing defender, arrows showing pass options, coach counting ball wins, clean infographic style, no text in image.

## Goalkeeper drills

### 19) Goalkeeper Reflexes — rapid-fire close range
**Measures:** Reflex saves/catches (out of 10)  
**Description:** Ten rapid close-range deliveries; GK attempts to save/catch; record successes out of 10.  
**AI prompt:** Goalkeeper reflex drill illustration, coach shooting or throwing balls rapidly from close range, goalkeeper reacting with quick saves, goal behind, clean coaching infographic style, no text in image.

### 20) Goalkeeper Handling — clean catches (shots + crosses)
**Measures:** Handling security (out of 10)  
**Description:** Mix of 5 shots and 5 crosses; record clean catches without spills out of 10.  
**AI prompt:** Goalkeeper handling drill diagram, split depiction of low shot catch and high cross catch, goalkeeper securing ball cleanly, coach feeding balls, clean instructional style, no text in image.

### 21) Goalkeeper Diving / Reach — top corner saves
**Measures:** Diving saves (out of 10)  
**Description:** Ten attempts (5 each side) aimed toward corners; record saves out of 10.  
**AI prompt:** Goalkeeper diving drill illustration, goalkeeper diving full-stretch toward top corner, ball traveling toward corner, cones/markers indicating target corners, clean coaching infographic style, no text in image.

### 22) Goalkeeper Distribution — drop-kick + throw into target grids
**Measures:** Distribution accuracy (out of 10)  
**Description:** 5 drop-kicks to a 40m target grid + 5 throws to a 20m target grid; record deliveries landing in target out of 10.  
**AI prompt:** Goalkeeper distribution drill diagram, goalkeeper drop-kicking ball toward distant target grid marked by cones at 40m, and throwing toward closer target grid at 20m, arrows showing trajectories, coach tracking accuracy, clean instructional style, no text in image.
