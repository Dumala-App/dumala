# Assessment drill guide prompts

This file lists every drill players may need to perform in the FPRDS Assessment Center, including:
- What it measures
- Coach-facing description (concise)
- A rich, instructional AI image prompt you can paste into your text-to-image model

Prompt style assumptions:
- “Clean minimal sports coaching infographic / training diagram”
- Cones, markers, distances, and movement arrows clearly visible; minimal background clutter
- Athlete pose shows the key action; coach shown timing/feeding/scoring where relevant
- No brand logos, no readable text or numbers inside the image

> The prompts below are derived directly from the `setup` and `procedure` fields
> in `src/fprds/drillGuide.ts`, so each illustration reflects how the drill is
> actually run.

## Base drills (all players)

### 1) 10m / 30m Sprint Test
**Measures:** Pace / acceleration (10m split + 30m time, best of 3)
**Description:** From a standing start, sprint at maximum effort through a 10m split and a 30m finish on a flat, straight lane; record the 10m split and 30m total time.
**AI prompt:** Clean minimal sports coaching infographic, side view of a flat grass soccer pitch, a straight 30-meter sprint lane running left to right, three cone markers on the ground at START, 10-meter split, and 30-meter finish, one athlete in a standing-start stance accelerating into a mid-sprint drive posture along the lane, a bold forward-pointing motion arrow following the lane, a coach standing just beyond the finish line holding a stopwatch, open safe run-off space past the finish, bright daylight, instructional diagram style, no readable text or logos in image.

### 2) Yo-Yo Intermittent Recovery Test
**Measures:** Stamina / endurance (total distance in meters)
**Description:** Run repeated 20m shuttles between two lines in time with a beep track, continuing until you can no longer reach the line on the beep; record total distance achieved.
**AI prompt:** Clean minimal sports coaching infographic, top-down view of a grass pitch with two parallel lines exactly 20 meters apart marked by cones, one athlete running a shuttle between the lines, a double-headed back-and-forth arrow along the shuttle path showing repeated runs, a coach at the side holding a phone or speaker playing a beep track, bright daylight, instructional diagram style, no readable text or logos in image.

### 3) Illinois Agility Test
**Measures:** Agility / change of direction (completion time, best of 2)
**Description:** Complete the standard Illinois agility cone course at maximum speed, weaving through the central cones and rounding the corners; record completion time.
**AI prompt:** Clean minimal sports coaching infographic, top-down view of the standard Illinois agility test layout on a grass pitch, a rectangular course marked by corner cones with a line of four slalom cones down the center, a continuous curved path arrow showing the athlete weaving through the slalom and rounding each corner, one athlete mid-turn leaning into a change of direction, a coach at the start-finish corner holding a stopwatch, bright daylight, instructional diagram style, no readable text or logos in image.

### 4) Standing Broad Jump + Medicine Ball Throw
**Measures:** Strength / power (broad jump distance + throw distance, best of 3 each)
**Description:** Two-footed standing broad jump from a takeoff line measured to the nearest heel; plus a consistent-technique medicine ball throw from behind a line measured for distance.
**AI prompt:** Clean minimal sports coaching infographic, split-panel diagram on a grass pitch; left panel shows an athlete performing a two-footed standing broad jump forward from a marked takeoff line with a measuring tape laid on the ground to the landing point and a forward arc arrow for the jump; right panel shows the same athlete throwing a medicine ball forward from behind a marked line with a measuring tape on the ground and an arc arrow for the throw; a coach observing with a clipboard, bright daylight, instructional diagram style, no readable text or logos in image.

### 5) Rebound Board Control / Wall Pass Grid

## Forwards / Wingers drills

### 7) Finishing (Box) — 6-ball rapid fire

### 9) Offensive 1v1 — attack channel
**Measures:** Dribbling / take-ons (successful take-ons out of 10)

### 10) Attacking Heading — target zones
